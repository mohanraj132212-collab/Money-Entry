Money Entry — Quick Guide

Core Concept & Balance Logic

Money Entry records money given to individuals and tracks how much balance remains to be returned.

  • Who: Name of the person who received money.
  • Reason: Description or purpose of transaction.
  • Amount: Total money given (e.g. ₹500).
  • Balance: Remaining amount that should come back to you (e.g. ₹150).
  • Validation: Balance can never exceed Amount.

Features & Storage

  • Firebase Firestore: All records are synchronized in real-time.
  • PDF Export: Generate printable statements for all entries or filtered search results.
  • Responsive Experience: Dedicated desktop sidebar navigation and mobile-optimized bottom navigation.
